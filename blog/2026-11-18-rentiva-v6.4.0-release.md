6.4.0 redesigns the pages a visitor sees first and makes the price on screen the price charged. The search strip, the results page and the vehicle card follow the new front-end design, with a category filter, filter chips, a comparison bar and a filter drawer on phones. With search dates, every card shows the total for the rental. The booking form quotes exactly what WooCommerce charges: seasonal pricing now reaches the payment, and taxes come only from WooCommerce. My Account follows the new design too. **Rentiva now needs WordPress 7.0 or newer, and you must clear your page cache after updating** — see the boxes below. It is published on WordPress.org, so WordPress offers it to you as an ordinary update; the WordPress.org page now has eight screenshots. **Using Rentiva Pro?** Update Pro to 6.5.0 after this release; Pro 6.4.x keeps working until then.

<!--truncate-->

:::caution Rentiva now requires WordPress 7.0 or newer
It was 6.7. The test suite was not green on WordPress 6.7 or 6.9, and the current WooCommerce requires 7.0 as well. Sites on an older WordPress keep 6.3.1 until they update WordPress.
:::

:::caution Clear your page cache after updating
Clear your host's cache, LiteSpeed Cache, WP Rocket and the like. A page cached by 6.3.x keeps the old booking form and calendar markup, which the new scripts do not drive, until the cache is cleared. Rentiva's own short-lived cache starts afresh on its own.
:::

:::caution If your theme overrides Rentiva templates
Templates under `templates/shortcodes/*` and `templates/partials/*` (booking form, availability calendar, contact form, vehicle details, rating form and search) have new markup. A theme that overrides them keeps its old markup, which the new scripts do not drive: remove or update those overrides after updating. A theme that overrides `templates/account/*.php` keeps its old markup and may look unstyled, because the old account stylesheets no longer load on these pages. A theme that overrides WooCommerce's `myaccount/dashboard.php` keeps its own.
:::

## A new search strip and results page

- **The search strip is one row:** pickup location, pickup date and time, return date (the return time follows the pickup time) and Search, with visible labels. It opens on the visitor's search, or tomorrow 10:00 to three days later (longer if the minimum rental is longer) when dates are required. There are no tabs: the "Show Rental Tab" option is removed from the block and the Elementor widget, and a saved value is ignored. With one pickup location, the strip shows it as a line of text instead of a list.
- **The results page has a filter column** with a Category filter, removable chips for active filters, a line with the number of vehicles, the dates and the number of days, sorting by total price when dates are set, a grid/list switch and page buttons. Changing a filter, the sort or the page keeps the dates and every filter.
- **On phones** the results page shows a summary card with a "Change" link and opens the filters in a full-screen drawer that works with the keyboard.
- **A comparison bar** shows how many vehicles are picked (up to 3), their names and a link to the comparison page. The comparison shortcode compares up to 3 vehicles (was 4).
- **Empty results explain why:** no dates chosen yet, no cars free for the dates, the filters left nothing, or no vehicles listed.
- **A pickup time that has already passed** can no longer be priced or booked by a customer. The search says so and lists the cars without dates. An administrator can still record a rental after the fact.
- **With page caching for visitors,** the cached results page depends on every filter, the page number, the dates and the visitor's comparison list, so one visitor's results are not shown to another. The filter options are refreshed when a vehicle is saved.
- **Dates fit their fields.** With WordPress's default date format ("September 30, 2026") a date no longer runs out of its field in the search strip or the vehicle page's booking card. The strip wraps by the width it is given, not the screen: each field keeps room for a long date, and one that does not fit moves to the next row. In Twenty Twenty-Five's content column (a 565px strip) the pickup and return each take a row with Search below; the strip is one row from about 800px wide (1000px with a pickup location). On phones the time sits under its date. In the usual 340px booking card each date takes a row beside its time (under it on phones). The strip keeps its width inside Row and Stack blocks and Elementor row containers.

## A redesigned vehicle card

- **The card is redesigned** on vehicle grids, lists, featured vehicles, search results and My Account > Favorites. With search dates it shows the total for the rental, required add-ons included, and the price per day. It lists seats, transmission and fuel, the deposit and the free-cancellation window. Elementor vehicle-card style settings keep working, and on update Elementor's generated CSS is rebuilt once so saved vehicle-card styles apply to the new card.
- **The total adds up.** A card with search dates names the required services its total includes and what they add ("Required service included: Full insurance $25.00").
- **Cards in one row match.** They lay out their price and Book button the same way, decided by the card's width: on a narrow card the price sits above the button, on a wide one beside it. Before, a price only a pixel wider (1,750.00 next to 850.00) pushed one card's button below while its neighbours kept it beside the price.
- **Also fixed:** the booking form shows the vehicle's details (seats, transmission, fuel) under its name again, the vehicle-details page's main photo fills its frame instead of showing only its top on Elementor pages, and featured-vehicle slider cards are all as tall as the tallest.

## Booking form and price

- **The booking form quotes exactly what is charged.** Seasonal pricing, when enabled, applies to the payment and the stored booking total, not only to the on-screen quote. The deposit and the remaining amount are worked out from the same total.
- **Taxes come only from WooCommerce.** Prices on the booking form are shown through WooCommerce's tax display (the "Display prices in the cart and checkout" setting and the price display suffix). Rentiva no longer draws its own tax line, and its own tax settings (Vehicle settings > Tax Inclusive Pricing and Tax Rate (%)) are removed. Taxes are configured in WooCommerce > Settings > Tax. The tax charged does not change: WooCommerce has always calculated it. Existing bookings keep their stored totals.
- **Pickup and return times are stored correctly.** A booking made through WooCommerce checkout stored its pickup and return as the local time read as UTC, so on a site outside UTC it held the vehicle for the wrong hours (three hours off in Istanbul) and a late-evening booking could overlap another. New bookings store the real time, and a one-time step on update corrects existing bookings, those in the trash included.
- **Date errors say what is wrong.** A return date before (or on) the pickup is answered with "Return date must be after pickup date." instead of "Invalid date/time format.", on the booking form, the price, the availability check, the manual booking screen and the search; the form keeps "Book" locked until the dates are fixed.
- **Optional rental agreement.** Under Settings > Booking > Rental agreement (off by default). When it is required, the customer ticks a consent box under the agreement before booking, and the server refuses a booking without it. The time and the version of the text accepted are stored with the booking and shown in its Customer Account box. An empty text uses a built-in, translatable template: a starting point, not legal advice.
- **Contact shortcode:** a `[rentiva_contact type="booking"]` or `type="feedback"` shortcode that does not set `show_topic_selector` keeps its fixed form with the preferred date or rating field, as in 6.3.1. Blocks and Elementor widgets follow their "Show Topic Selector" toggle, which is on by default: turn it off to keep a fixed booking or feedback form.
- **Smaller:** a page with both the booking form and the availability calendar no longer starts the form's availability line as a second calendar. The "Classic cart page" setting (Settings > Payment) replaces the Cart block only when the cart holds a rental, and keeps that cart classic until it is emptied; a cart with only shop products keeps the Cart block and its express payment buttons.

## Availability calendars

- **A partly free day is shown.** The return day of a booking is free from the return time plus the buffer, the pickup day of the next one until its pickup time minus the buffer (half-shaded, with the time). A day the operator closed by hand reads "Closed" instead of "Booked". Picking a return day as the start moves the pickup time there and says so.
- **The calendar shows 2 months by default** (was 1); an explicit `months_to_show` still applies.
- **A calendar whose `vehicle_id` names a vehicle that does not exist or cannot be rented** shows "Vehicle not found." instead of another vehicle's calendar.
- **On phones** the vehicle page's availability legend (Selected, Partly available, Booked, Closed) wraps onto a second line instead of running past the edge of the page.

## My Account

- **My Account follows the new design.** The dashboard greets the customer and shows the next rental; the booking page shows progress, payment, when free cancellation ends and how much is refunded. The dashboard keeps showing a rental picked up earlier today as the next rental instead of "You have no upcoming rentals."
- **My bookings is split** into Upcoming, Past and Cancelled (each with its count; the section is in the address as `mhmrentiva_view`). It shows two full dates and the booking number customers see in e-mails, and becomes one card per booking on phones. The menu shows the number of upcoming bookings and saved vehicles. The old filter box and search field are gone; links that carry their values still narrow the list. My favorites uses the same layout.
- **Each section has its own order:** Upcoming soonest pickup first, Past and Cancelled latest first. Saved blocks and widgets that used the old default order follow it too. The new "Automatic" order option is the default; an order chosen explicitly still applies.
- **Menu names:** "My bookings", "My favorites" and "Payment history". WooCommerce's own items are unchanged.
- **A printable Booking summary,** from the dashboard, the booking page and the thank-you page. It is not an invoice.
- **"Upload bank receipt."** The customer's file upload is called that now. It appears, and the server accepts or removes a file, only while a booking awaits payment or confirmation and no money has been received. Staff can still remove a file. In Turkish the receipt is called "dekont" everywhere.
- **Payment history** shows the car name, the amount actually paid, and the right status for a paid booking.
- **Smaller:** "Withdraw Consent" in the privacy controls appears only once the customer has given consent, and "Clear all favorites" no longer shows an error message before it runs. A page carrying the retired customer dashboard (shortcode, block or Elementor widget) showed a guest an empty page anywhere but the plugin's own /panel/ page; it now shows the "Your account has moved" note with a link to My Account, where the login form is.
- **With Rentiva Pro 6.4.x,** the transfer add-on window shows the currency next to its amounts again.
- **Typeface:** the new front-end pages use Plus Jakarta Sans, served from your own site (the same font file the admin screens already used). The unused Source Serif 4 font files are no longer shipped.

## Cancellations, refunds and payments

- **The free-cancellation deadline** used the wrong pickup time on bookings created by hand; fixed.
- **Refunds from the Payment Management box.** A refund from the booking's Payment Management box was refused "due to cancellation policy" from one day after the booking was made, however far away pickup was. The cancellation deadline now counts from pickup everywhere: the box shows that deadline, and refunding a cancelled booking pays its refundable balance. The box no longer offers to cancel a booking that is already cancelled, completed or refunded. The `mhmrentiva_cancellation_deadline_hours` filter, which only set that wrong one-day value on bookings created by hand, is removed; the deadline is set in Settings > Booking > Cancellation Deadline (Hours).
- **Remaining-balance payments.** A failed or cancelled remaining-balance payment no longer cancels the booking. A remaining payment that no longer matches the booking's balance does not clear it silently: the balance is kept and the booking is flagged for staff, with an order note. Only the booking's current payment link can take the remaining payment.
- **Bookings paid outside the site.** A booking entered by hand and paid outside the site (cash or card at pickup) is no longer cancelled automatically after the payment deadline or once its pickup day has passed, and the customer is no longer told the payment window expired. A hand-entered booking paid online keeps its deadline.
- **A full refund from the WooCommerce order screen** before the rental started (confirmed, pending or awaiting payment) kept holding the car: its payment showed refunded while the booking stayed active. It is now cancelled, which frees its dates. A completed rental refunded in full is still marked refunded, and a rental in progress keeps its dates, since the car is out. The customer gets one e-mail for it, the refund notice, not a second cancellation notice.
- **An Update that changes only the note, the status or another field that does not set the price** no longer shows the "vehicle, dates and add-ons were not changed" warning on a booking whose stored total differs from today's rates.

## Admin, languages and accessibility

- **Customers and Contact Messages tables** are named for screen readers and show a partly selected page on the select-all box. The Customers table sorts through a real button that announces the sorted column. mhm/ui-core is 0.20.1.
- **The admin notice about a WooCommerce Cart or Checkout block can be put away:** "Got it, do not show this again" hides it for that administrator on every screen.
- **Personal data export and erasure include bookings in the trash.** A trashed booking still holds the customer's data until the trash is emptied; it is anonymised in place and stays in the trash.
- **Numbers follow the page's language.** Vehicle mileage and engine size are written the way the page's language writes numbers: an English page shows "18,500 km" and "1.6 L", a Turkish page "18.500 km" and "1,6 L". Before, mileage was always written with Turkish separators and engine size always with English ones.
- **Sites in more than one language (Polylang, WPML)** show the vehicle page's availability message and the search sidebar's fuel, transmission and category labels in the visitor's language. Both answers were cached for every visitor in the first visitor's language, so a Turkish "already booked" could reach the English page. The caches now hold no text, and entries cached before this release are reworded too.
- **Three Turkish translations** start lower-case like their originals.
- **Security:** the REST nonce is no longer printed on every page. It is printed on My Account pages, on pages with the My bookings or Payment history shortcode and on pages with the search form.
- **Removed:** the dashboard no longer reads the unregistered options `mhmrentiva_customer_dashboard_welcome`, `mhmrentiva_customer_favorites` and `mhmrentiva_customer_booking_history`. No settings screen wrote them; a value set by hand has no effect any more.

## What to check after updating

- **WordPress version:** it must be 7.0 or newer.
- **Page cache:** clear it.
- **Theme overrides:** remove or update overrides of the booking form, calendar, contact form, vehicle details, rating form, search and account templates.
- **Taxes:** Rentiva's own tax settings are gone; check WooCommerce > Settings > Tax and the price display setting.
- **Contact forms:** shortcodes, blocks and widgets that use `type="booking"` or `type="feedback"` behave as described above.
- **Rentiva Pro:** update Pro to 6.5.0.
