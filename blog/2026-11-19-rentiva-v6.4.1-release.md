6.4.1 is a small update that fixes what an admin reads on three screens and what a new customer receives. The bookings calendar summary now shows the pickup and return times, the total with your shop's currency and dates in your site's format. Upcoming Operations on the dashboard writes its dates in your admin language. A customer whose account WooCommerce creates gets one welcome e-mail instead of two. Nothing to configure, no database step. **Using Rentiva Pro?** Nothing to do: Pro 6.5.1 works with 6.4.1.

<!--truncate-->

## One welcome e-mail for a new account

When WooCommerce created a customer's account, at checkout or in My Account, the customer received two welcomes: WooCommerce's own new-account e-mail and Rentiva's "Welcome to …" e-mail. Now Rentiva's welcome is skipped for these accounts, so the customer gets one.

If you have switched WooCommerce's new-account e-mail off (**WooCommerce > Settings > Emails**), Rentiva's welcome is still sent, so nobody is left without one. Customers whose accounts were created before this update are not changed.

## The bookings calendar summary

Click a booked day on **Bookings > Calendar** to open the summary.

- **Pickup and return times** now appear under the dates. They were always stored with the booking, but the summary showed "—".
- **The total** carries your shop's currency and is formatted as in the bookings list (for example $237.60 instead of 237.6).
- **Dates and the created time** follow your site's date and time format (**Settings > General**).

On a day with several bookings, the list in the summary shows each booking's dates with their times.

## Dates on the dashboard

- **Upcoming Operations** writes its dates in your admin language. Before, it used the browser's language, so an English admin screen in a Turkish browser read "20 Kas".
- A booking no longer shows **one day early** when your browser is west of UTC (for example in the Americas).

## Smaller fixes

- The **blocked-days list** on the vehicle editor no longer stops working for an admin whose language is Portuguese (AO90).
- A **fresh install** no longer writes a "Table … doesn't exist" database error to the log.
